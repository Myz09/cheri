FAQ

Do I need prior coding experience?
No. Our resources are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to those with programming experience.
Does tutoring cost money?
All resources are free. Cherielle is dedicated to making computer science education accessible to everyone.
Can non-AP students join?
Yes. While our focus is on AP CSA preparation, we welcome anyone interested in learning computer science and exploring tech resources.
When will review sessions begin?
Announcements will be shared after curriculum finalization. Sign up for the interest list to stay notified of updates.

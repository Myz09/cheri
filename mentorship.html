Mentorship at Cherielle

Our 3-month virtual mentorship program brings together female and gender-expansive students with experienced mentors in computer science. It is designed for all experience levels, including complete beginners.

Why this program exists

Support for students and mentors

If you are a student trying to figure out if tech is right for you, or if you are ready to be the role model you wish you had when you were younger, this is the perfect opportunity.

  • Low commitment: 1 hour minimum per month
  • Virtual meetings through Zoom, Google Meet, Instagram, Discord, or another platform
  • Helpful discussion prompts each month
  • Open to beginners and experienced students alike

Program details

What to expect

We run 3-month cycles for each pairing and carefully match participants to help create a thoughtful and supportive connection.

  • Rolling applications with no deadline
  • Mentors and mentees may continue contact after the program if both want to
  • Orientation with both parties once a match is made
  • Volunteer hours and a certificate are available for eligible participants

Who can participate

General requirements

We welcome anyone who wants to find a mentor, be a role model, earn volunteer hours, or gain a resume booster. The program is intended for female and gender-expansive students and mentors who are passionate about making space for more voices in tech.

  • Mentees: Female or gender-expansive, age 13+, located anywhere, and open to learning about computer science.
  • Mentors: Female or gender-expansive university students or professionals with experience in tech and computer science.
  • Communication: Participants must be able to stay in contact through email or another social platform.

We ask that all participants commit to at least one hour of communication per month and maintain respectful, reliable contact with their partner.

FAQ

Do I need coding experience to join?
No. The program is open to beginners and people who are simply curious about computer science.
Is the program free?
Yes. It is a community-based opportunity designed to be accessible and supportive.
How do volunteer hours work?
Participants may log their hours and provide evidence such as screenshots of meeting duration so the team can verify them.

Past Events

October 2025
Codeshield Workshop with Medisec
In October of 2025, Cherielle collaborated with Medisec to host a workshop focused on the intersection of cybersecurity and healthcare with 30+ participants over the span of two weekends. Cherielle's team organised and managed workshop logistics effectively in order to conduct multiple coding activities and interactive investigations. Through these activities, participants gained useful coding and problem solving skills. Participants also learned important information about the role of cybersecurity in healthcare. Through this event both Cherielle and Medisec received strong online engagement with the shared Instagram post reaching 100+ likes and through this we increased awareness about cybersecurity issues particularly relating to healthcare systems.
August 2025
Joint Post with Lumora
In August of 2025, Cherielle collaborated with Lumora and created a post on Instagram called "Where AI Can Take You". This post discussed the fields and industries that are changing with AI and how people can use the technology to help with tasks and increase productivity. Cherielle researched information for and designed three of the six slides with meaningful details and insights to educate our audience. This post was targeted at middle and high schoolers to better inform them about how to use AI and new technology to their advantage but also be mindful and appropriate about their usage.
July 2025
Joint Post with WA STEMHER
In July of 2025, Cherielle collaborated with Washington Stem-Her to create an Instagram post called "All About Interdisciplinary Computer Science", highlighting how computer science can be a useful tool to combine with other fields. Cherielle's team worked on designing and researching information for five slides that discussed the positive aspects that could be gained from applying computer science to other industries and provided deeper examples of how computer science can be used in specific fields. This post was targeted at middle and high school students to inform them about how computer science knowledge is an important skill that they can put to use with other specializations through interdisciplinary computer science.
February 2025
Next Gen Tech Workshop Collab
In December of 2025, Cherielle hosted a workshop with 10+ participants in partnership with Next Gen Tech with the goal of providing digital literacy training for beginners. To achieve this, Cherielle's team designed informational presentations on fundamental digital software use and rudimentary Python for beginner participants. The team then encouraged the participants to use their recently acquired knowledge and skills by creating an interactive game project on Rock Paper Scissors. Through this event, Cherielle helped participants develop foundational skills in Python and digital software by creating a supportive and encouraging learning environment that promoted collaboration. On Instagram, we received much engagement with 140+ likes and several comments on posts and reels.
January 2025
Pinkgap Medicine Conference II
In January of 2025, Cherielle partnered with 9 other non-profit organizations to address medical issues in a conference. The conference featured a total of 12 speakers and had 250 people in attendance, with the conference also being recorded and published on Youtube to reach a larger audience. Cherielle specifically explored the role of wearable tech in the medical field by creating a presentation and featuring 2 representative speakers. Through this Cherielle shared informative insights and promoted volunteer opportunities to the attendees. The event also gained substantial engagement on social media with the Instagram posts reaching 200 likes and 60 comments collectively.
November 2024
Hour of Code Workshop with Pinkgap
In November of 2024, Cherielle worked with Pinkgap to host a workshop targeted at specifically middle school students to introduce them to python basics. The workshop consisted of 10 participants who worked towards a final project showcase where they could present the final project they built. Participants were also offered the chance to earn a $25 Amazon gift card and a $60 grant. Social media engagement consisted of 150 likes and 50 comments across all Instagram posts made on the topic.

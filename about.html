Bridging the Gender Gap

The gender gap in is real, not just in tech but in general as well. There have been many advancements to reduce this disparity, yet even today, there are always instances of girls feeling undermined in the classroom and unmotivated to pursue their dreams. We aim to create blossoms of impact starting from locally to out in the web. We're the girl's girl - by creating a safe, inclusive community bonded by computer science and girlhood, we have a space to connect, grow, and learn.

Providing accessible resources

We believe that every girl deserves an opportunity in STEM, which is why we offer a variety of free resources, including: tutoring in Java, Python, and HTML/CSS/JS, curricula for AP CSP and AP CSA, free CS resources, workshops, contests, volunteer, and mentorship opportunities. It doesn't matter if you're a beginner, or if you already have experience with coding; we have resources designed for people of all different kinds of CS backgrounds!

Community empowerment

We aim to build an inclusive community that embodies the intersection of computer science and girlhood, where girls can connect, grow, and learn together. Through workshops, hackathons, collaborations, and creative projects such as our zine, our goal is to create a space where girls of all backgrounds and skill levels can feel safe and supported, and to see themselves as an important part of the CS field. We understand how tech can be a lonely place when you're the only girl in the room, and nobody should have to do this alone.
